A note from me

Why I vlog

Films are planned. Vlogs aren’t. That’s exactly why I love them.

I started vlogging because so much of what I care about happens off-script — the night before a competition, the walk to the venue, the hug after a performance. None of that ends up in a finished film, but it’s often the part I remember most.

Vlogging keeps me honest. There’s no second take and no hiding behind a crew; it’s just me, a camera and whatever the day decides to be. It’s made me a quicker shooter, a better listener and a lot less afraid of pointing a lens at real life.

Mostly, these are for the people in them. If you were there, I hope this brings it back.

— Your Name
