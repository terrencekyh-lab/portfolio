Scene 02

The person behind the camera

I fell in love with film the night I stayed up way too late watching something that made me feel less alone. I’ve been chasing that feeling with a camera ever since.

I write, direct, shoot and edit — mostly short films, music videos and the occasional documentary about people I find quietly fascinating. I’m drawn to stories about growing up, small towns, and the moments right before something changes.

When I’m not filming, I’m probably taking photos on a camera older than me, over-analysing a movie with friends, or rearranging my room to look like a set.

Currently

  • Watching Every film by Wong Kar-wai, in order
  • Shooting on Sony a6400 + a 50mm I refuse to replace
  • Writing A short about my grandmother’s kitchen
  • Learning Colour grading in DaVinci Resolve

Films that made me
